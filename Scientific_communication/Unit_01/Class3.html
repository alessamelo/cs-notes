The Declaration on Science and the Use of Scientific Knowledge

The Declaration on Science and the Use of Scientific Knowledge was adopted at the UNESCO-sponsored World Conference on Science in 1999.

Main principles

  1. Scientific knowledge should be shared.
  2. There is a need for real cooperation between governments, civil society, the business sector, and scientists.
  3. Scientists should be governed by ethical standards.
  4. Professionals who circulate scientific information should see communication as an important responsibility.

Scientific Communication

Difusión

Transmitir el conocimiento científico con la comunidad científica.

Divulgación

Transmitir el conocimiento científico a personas fuera de la comunidad científica.

Features and Goals

The goals of technology and science aim to improve human quality of life.

There are socioeconomic gaps between different sectors of the population.

Bibliographic References

Important: It is important that paragraphs have two bibliographic references.

What Is Scientific Disclosure For?

Scientific disclosure tries to reduce the educational gap among different sectors of the population.

It aims to communicate scientific knowledge in a way that can be understood by people outside the scientific community.

Storytelling in Scientific Communication

Communicating science should follow a storytelling approach.

Using excessive niche terminology can make scientific information difficult to understand, which is not the goal of scientific disclosure.

Key Concepts

  • Scientific diffusion: communication of scientific knowledge within the scientific community.
  • Scientific disclosure: communication of scientific knowledge to people outside the scientific community.
  • Scientific communication: a way of transmitting scientific knowledge clearly and responsibly.
  • Storytelling: a useful approach for making scientific communication easier to understand.
